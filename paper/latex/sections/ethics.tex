The procedure records are public administrative information. The National Public Service Portal has no official API; records were
collected once (13 October 2026) through the portal's public, unauthenticated JSON endpoints used by its own web pages, at two requests per
second on a single thread, without logging in or bypassing any access control, and contain no personal data. Citizen questions come from two
public Hugging Face datasets whose cards state no license; personal data were masked before labeling, no names or identifiers appear in this
paper, and no data were collected from human participants, so no ethics approval was required. Because the licenses are unstated, we do not redistribute the question texts. The
code, the locked question identifiers and hashes, the labels and the per-question decisions of every run, without question texts, are
available in the project repository \todo{URL}; each identifier maps to a row of the original datasets.
