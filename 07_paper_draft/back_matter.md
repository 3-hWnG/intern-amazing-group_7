## Ethics and Data Availability

The procedure records are public, were collected without logging in (Sec. 3) and contain no personal data. The citizen questions come from public datasets whose cards state no license; personal data were masked before labeling, no names or identifiers appear in this paper, and no data were collected from human participants, so no ethics approval was required. We use them only for evaluation and do not redistribute the question texts. The code, the locked question identifiers and hashes, the labels and the per-question decisions of every run are at <https://github.com/3-hWnG/intern-amazing-group_7/tree/paper1-vjcs-draft>; each identifier maps to a row of the original datasets, and the full commit history is available to reviewers on request.

## Declaration of AI Use

During the preparation of this work, the authors used Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic) to assist with writing and modifying the software, running and summarizing the experiments, first-pass annotation of the development and first test sets (Sec. 3), and drafting and language editing of the manuscript; the code that builds the procedure database was first written with Claude Opus 5 and later modified with Claude Opus 5.5. The same assistant that produced the first-pass labels also wrote the rules evaluated here. Gemini 4 Argon (Google) proposed the labels of the second-pass sample and of the second test set, which the project lead (the first author) reviewed item by item. The research questions, the evaluation protocol and the final decisions were made by the authors, who reviewed, verified and edited the output and take full responsibility for the content of the publication.
