The procedure records are public, were collected without logging in (Sec.~\ref{sec:data})
and contain no personal data. The citizen questions come from public datasets whose cards state no license; personal data were
masked before labeling, no names or identifiers appear in this paper, and no data were collected from human participants, so no ethics
approval was required. We use them only for evaluation and do not redistribute the question texts. The code, the locked question identifiers and hashes, the labels and the
per-question decisions of every run are at \url{https://github.com/3-hWnG/intern-amazing-group_7/tree/paper1-vjcs-draft}; each identifier maps to a row
of the original datasets, and the full commit history is available to reviewers on request.
